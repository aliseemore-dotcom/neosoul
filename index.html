Meet Anastasija

London-based founder, creative thinker, and someone who has always been a little obsessed with understanding people.

Before you begin

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know anything about tarot?

No. Anastasija will explain the cards and their relevance to your question in clear, simple language.

What if I cannot clearly explain my question?

That is completely fine. Describe what is happening and what feels difficult. Anastasija can help clarify the focus of the consultation.

How many questions can I ask?

This depends on the format. The exact number or scope of questions is shown on each consultation above, before you book.

When will I receive my written reading?

Your PDF will be sent within delivery time after payment and receipt of your completed form.

Can I change the time of my live consultation?

Yes, subject to the cancellation and rescheduling policy. Please get in touch at least notice period before your appointment.

Is my information private?

Yes. Your question, personal details and consultation are treated as confidential and are not shared with third parties unless required for payment or service delivery.
